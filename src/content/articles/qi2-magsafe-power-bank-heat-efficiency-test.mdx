**Every airport gift shop and Amazon deal page is packed with slim 5,000mAh and 10,000mAh magnetic wireless power banks promising to "double your iPhone or Android battery life without cables." Then you snap one onto your phone at 15% battery, and two hours later the pack is dead while your phone is warm to the touch and sitting at 74%.**

Where did the rest of the electricity go? Straight into warming up your palm. Inductive wireless charging across two glass-and-case air gaps is fundamentally a miniature induction heater attached directly to your smartphone's lithium-ion pouch cell.

To quantify the real-world penalty, we discharged the top-selling **Qi2 15W magnetic power banks** from **Anker** and **Ugreen** twice: once purely over the 15W magnetic wireless coil, and once using a 6-inch braided USB-C cable connected to the exact same pack.

## Wired vs. Wireless: The 35% Inductive Heat Tax

A 10,000mAh portable battery stores roughly **37.0 Watt-hours (Wh)** inside its internal 3.7V lithium-polymer cells. When you plug a short USB-C cable into the pack's 30W port, the DC-to-DC boost converter operates at **86% to 88% net efficiency**, delivering **32.1 Wh** directly to your phone while keeping battery temperatures under **34.5°C**.

Switch to the 15W Qi2 magnetic coil on the exact same power bank, and two separate inefficiencies stack on top of each other: DC-to-AC inverter switching losses in the transmitter coil, and eddy-current heat inside the phone's receiver coil. Total net energy delivered drops to **23.8 Wh**—a **35.6% loss** from cell capacity.

Even more importantly, because the warm power bank is pressed flush against the back of your phone, it acts as a thermal blanket that traps heat inside the handset, causing the phone's PMIC to throttle charging speeds after 22 minutes.

## The Smart Way to Use a Magnetic Power Bank

The secret to getting your money's worth out of a magnetic Qi2 pack is using it as a **hybrid**: snap it onto the back of your iPhone or Pixel as a magnetic grip while using a **6-inch USB-C-to-USB-C jumper cable** plugged into its 30W port. You get the one-handed convenience of a magnetic attachment with **twice the charging speed**, **35% more usable battery capacity**, and **7°C cooler battery temperatures**.
