**Walk down the virtual aisle on Amazon today and you will see palm-sized Gallium Nitride (GaN) wall chargers boasting 140W, 200W, and even 240W ratings on the box. What the glossy infographics never show you is what happens at Minute 18 when you actually pull 200 Watts continuously.**

Even at a stellar 93% power conversion efficiency, a 200-Watt charger still has to dissipate **14 Watts of pure waste heat** inside a sealed plastic cube with zero airflow. That is more heat than a passive Raspberry Pi 5 locked inside an airtight thermos.

When we hooked today's most popular multi-port chargers from **Anker** and **Ugreen** up to our programmable DC electronic load bank at 22°C ambient, the internal thermal protection circuits revealed a clear divide between marketing peak numbers and continuous reality.

## Anker Prime vs. Ugreen Nexode: Oscilloscope & Thermal Results

In our 60-minute continuous torturous load test, **100W to 140W** proved to be the true physical ceiling for pocketable fold-prong wall bricks. Both the **Anker Prime 100W** and the **Ugreen Nexode Pro 100W** completed the full 60-minute run at 100% rated output without a single thermal step-down.

Where Anker edged ahead was **voltage ripple suppression** and **port-renegotiation speed**. On our oscilloscope, the Anker Prime 140W kept peak-to-peak output ripple under **68 mV** on the 28V rail, compared to **112 mV** on budget third-party GaN clones (well within the 300 mV USB-IF limit, but cleaner power reduces coil whine on laptop motherboards).

Ugreen's **Nexode 140W / 160W** series, meanwhile, delivered the best **watts-per-dollar value** whenever Amazon coupons drop it into the \$55–\$69 bracket, sustaining 132W continuously with a slightly larger surface-area chassis that breathes better on a desk.

## The Buyer's Rule for GaN Chargers & Cables

Skip the gimmicky 4-port and 6-port "200W pocket cubes" that overheat in twenty minutes. If you need a travel charger that never throttles, buy a **100W or 140W 3-port brick from Anker Prime or Ugreen Nexode**, and pair it with a certified **240W E-Marker braided USB-C cable** so your laptop actually negotiates full PD 3.1 speed.
